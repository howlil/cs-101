## Direction
Product: compact, research-grade cross-domain engineering library.
Character: neutral, text-forward and diagrammatic.
World: preserve existing monochrome tokens and Plus Jakarta Sans.
Moment: a focused concept, readable linked neighbors, and mapped real units.
Signature: semantic navigation instead of a force-directed graph canvas.

## Guardrails
No shadows, invented lessons, progress badges, new persistence, or duplicate sidebar.
## State
Mode and selected concept belong to URL query params.
## Patterns
Every connection is a native link. Mobile stacks the concept and its neighbors.
## Accessibility
Keyboard, visible focus, semantic labels and readable text alternatives are mandatory.
